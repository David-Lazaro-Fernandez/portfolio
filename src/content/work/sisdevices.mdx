## The goal

[Sis Devices](https://sisdevices.com) sells machine-safety equipment in Monterrey: light curtains, safety sensors, guards for lathes and drills, forklift safety gear, and lockout/tagout devices.

The project was a redesign of their old site into an online store. Customers needed to find a product fast, understand its technical specs, and buy it online, while the team needed to manage the catalog themselves.

## Design

Every screen was designed first: home, catalog, product, cart, checkout, and order confirmation. The look stays close to the brand, with yellow and black on a light background, the colors of safety signage.

The catalog is organized two ways, because customers shop both ways: by the machine they need to protect (forklifts, cranes, lathes, drills, saws) and by the type of device (safety relays, coded sensors, ATEX equipment, inductive and ultrasonic sensors).

Safety equipment is technical, so product pages go beyond a photo and a price. Each one has a spec table, a downloadable data sheet, and a technical zone that explains concepts like the minimum safety distance for a light curtain. A "talk to an expert" card lets customers book a call. For products that have to be sized to a machine, like the safety blocks for presses, the page includes a step-by-step quote form instead of a plain checkout.

## Build

I built the store with Next.js and TypeScript, styled with Tailwind CSS and shadcn/ui components, on top of Firebase:

- **Catalog and products** live in Firestore, with images in Firebase Storage. I migrated and cleaned up the existing product data into the new structure.
- **Search** opens as a modal with suggested searches and category shortcuts.
- **Accounts and cart**: customers sign up and sign in with Firebase Auth, and the cart persists across pages.
- **English and Spanish**: the whole site is translated with next-intl, with a language switch in the header.
- **Admin dashboard**: the team manages products (create, edit, upload images, and write descriptions in a rich text editor), with pages for orders, customers, and analytics.
- **Content pages**: services, safety norms, and an about page.

## Working with the client

We started from their old site and catalog and designed the full purchase flow before writing code, from browsing to the order confirmation. Then I built the store piece by piece between December 2024 and February 2025: the landing page, product pages, catalog, cart, translations, and finally the admin dashboard.
